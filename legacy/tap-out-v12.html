A wilderness survival game

Ten strangers. One rule.

You are dropped alone on the northern shoreline - no crew, no help, nothing but what you carry and a phone that calls a helicopter. Nine others are out there somewhere, each on a shoreline of their own. Whoever lasts the longest wins. Everyone else goes home - by pressing the button, by the medics' decision, or because the North decides for them.

Build. Eat. Stay warm. Stay sane.

Right now you are strong - weeks of good meals still in your blood. That strength is a loan. Within days, the land starts collecting.

The days will get shorter the longer this goes - the light itself is not on your side.

Talk to the camera. It's the only one listening.
