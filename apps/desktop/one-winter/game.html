TAP / OUT · One Winter

Outlast nine rivals before the North wins

DAY 1 · MORNING

Tip

Sound starts when you start a run. Your run is saved in this browser after every click.

How to play

  1. Pick 3 things for your pack. The choices change every run.
  2. Click things in the picture, or use the list in the corner. Use the names at the top to walk between Camp, Shore and Woods. Walking is free.
  3. Each day has three turns, then a card at night. Every day is colder. Last one in wins.
  4. Each win opens a harder season. Playing well unlocks new kit.

When you're done

A run takes about 20 to 30 minutes. Then reply in the chat with the one thing that bugged you most, or the one thing you liked.

Keys: 1 to 9 pick an action or a choice, C, S and W walk to Camp, Shore and Woods, M turns sound on or off, O opens settings.
