TAP / OUT · One Winter

Outlast nine rivals before the North wins

DAY 1 · MORNING

Tip

Sound starts when you start a run. Your run is saved in this browser after every click.

How to play

  1. Pick 10 of the 16 things for your pack.
  2. Click things in the picture, or use the list in the corner. Use the names at the top to walk between Camp, Shore and Woods.
  3. Every job takes time. Outside work stops at dark; the evening is for the fire. Every day is colder. Last one in wins.
  4. Each win opens a harder season. Playing well unlocks new kit.

When you're done

A run takes about 30 to 50 minutes. Then reply in the chat with the one thing that bugged you most, or the one thing you liked.

Keys: 1 to 9 pick an action or a choice, C, S and W walk to Camp, Shore and Woods, M turns sound on or off, O opens settings.
